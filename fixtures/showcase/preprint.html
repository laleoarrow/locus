Calibration Drift in Clinical Risk Models: A Multi-Site Analysis

Demo content for screenshots

Submitted 2026 · cs.LG

Abstract

Risk models deployed across several hospitals lose calibration faster than they lose discrimination. We quantify this gap and argue that recalibration schedules, not retraining, should be the default maintenance strategy for deployed models.

Discussion

Discrimination is the metric most often reported, yet it is calibration that decides whether a predicted probability can be acted on at the bedside. A model with unchanged AUC and drifted calibration will quietly mislead every threshold-based decision built on top of it.

We therefore recommend that deployment reports include a calibration curve for each site and each year, rather than a single pooled figure.
