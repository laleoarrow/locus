Anesthesiology. 2019 Dec; 131(6): 1346–1359. doi: 10.1097/ALN.0000000000002960

Methods

Reporting quality was assessed against established guidelines, with particular attention to whether the sample size, the handling of missing data, and the calibration of predicted probabilities were described in enough detail to be reproduced.

Models that were never tested outside their development institution should be described as hypothesis-generating rather than as clinically validated tools.
